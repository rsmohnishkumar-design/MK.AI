How to add an example (easy)

  1. Press Give me a question, or type your own school question.
  2. Find the answer: your textbook, notes, a teacher, or Gemini / Google to learn the fact.
  3. Close it, then type the answer in your own words: 1–3 short sentences, no bullet points.
  4. Press Add example. The admin checks it before NOVA learns it.

Why your own words? Copying Gemini's or ChatGPT's text to train another AI breaks their rules, and NOVA learns better from short, simple answers. Facts are fine; just write them yourself. No phone numbers, emails or anyone's private details.

    How training works

    1. Add and check examples here. The admin approves good ones.
    2. The admin exports the approved examples into nova-llm/data/contrib/.
    3. A trainer runs python pipeline.py --name yourname-1 on Kaggle or Colab (free GPU).
    4. Only a model that beats the live one on the fixed exams gets promoted.
