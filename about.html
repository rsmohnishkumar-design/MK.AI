About Nova AI

Nova AI is a free AI assistant for learning: maths, science, coding, general knowledge and Tamil culture. Its own model answers your questions, and it keeps learning from what people ask.

Models

AnswersAlpacaNova AI's model, in four modes. Instant: maths, science, conversions and its knowledge base of about 2,650 answers run right in your browser. Pro: writing, explaining, advice, code, apps and office files, using large language models such as Google Gemini. Live: news and anything happening now, with Google Search. Deep: careful step-by-step thinking. Every answer it looks up is saved to its memory, so it keeps getting smarter.
Pictures & videosAlpaca VisionMakes pictures and short videos from a description: HD pictures with Google's image model, realistic photos from a free volunteer-run image service, and logos, cards, charts and patterns drawn by Alpaca itself. For unlimited members; videos are made for the admin only.

Pick a model from the menu under the message box: Alpaca (fast, smart answers), Alpaca Pro (the big model for every answer), Alpaca Pro (Deep thinking) (thinks step by step and checks its work) and Alpaca Vision (pictures; videos are for the admin). The same menu sets the style: Assistant, Tutor, Coder, Writer, Career guide or Friend.

What it can do

  • Maths: arithmetic, fractions, percentages, algebra, equations, derivatives, integrals, limits, matrices, vectors, complex numbers and word problems, with step-by-step working.
  • Science: physics formulas with units, chemistry (molar mass, balancing equations), biology and space.
  • Coding: explanations, examples in many languages, 39 ready-to-run apps (including ATM, traffic light, elevator, vending machine, solar system and projectile simulators), any management system you describe ("make a library management system with book name, author and year" gives a working app with a form, search, sorting, totals and CSV export), and a Code School for beginners.
  • Knowledge: countries, India and Tamil Nadu, history, people, inventions, festivals, careers, full forms, important days, proverbs and the Thirukkural.
  • Photos and documents: attach a photo, a PDF (homework, a bill, a notice) or a text or code file and ask about it, or say "summarise:" and paste a long text.
  • YouTube and web pages: paste a link and ask for a summary, notes or answers from it.
  • Study packs: notes, flashcards, practice questions and an answer key for any topic and class.
  • Run code in the chat: Python and JavaScript run right under the code; HTML apps open in a preview. If something fails, Fix with Alpaca corrects it.
  • Personalities and voice chat: from the ⋯ menu, pick Tutor, Coder, Writer, Career guide, Friend, Wellbeing support or Money help, or talk hands-free.
  • Quizzes: "quiz me on capitals", "quiz me on full forms": multiple-choice questions with instant marking.
  • Feels like a real assistant: answers are typed out, spelling slips are corrected ("Did you mean…?"), and explanations offer more detail or simpler.
  • Office files: "make a ppt on the solar system", "write a word document on pollution", "make an excel sheet for student marks": Nova AI researches the topic, shows a preview, and gives a real .pptx, .docx or .xlsx (Excel sheets come with working formulas).
  • AI voiceovers: "make a voiceover: Welcome to our annual day…" or "make a voiceover about the water cycle" (Nova AI writes the script). Choose a voice, a style (narrator, energetic, calm, deep, cartoon), speed and pitch; on a computer, Record saves the narration as an audio file. The voices come from your device.
  • Talks like a friend: feelings and advice (with helplines if someone is in distress), small talk, stories, riddles, fun facts, quotes, poems, games and everyday choices.
  • Remembers you: tell it your name, class or what you like, and it greets you by name and tailors Alpaca's answers to you. This is saved on your device only.
  • Coding studio: tap Edit on any code to open it full screen, run it, and ask Alpaca to change it in plain words (with Undo). ✦ Alpaca on code explains it, finds bugs, improves it, adds comments, writes tests or converts it to another language.
  • Diagrams: ask for a flowchart, mind map, timeline or sequence diagram ("draw a flowchart of the water cycle") and Alpaca draws it in the chat, ready to download as an SVG picture. SVG code is shown as a picture too.
  • Quick commands and shortcuts: type / in the box for /code, /debug, /explain, /diagram, /quiz, /notes, /translate, /deep and more. On a keyboard: / to type, Ctrl+K to search chats, Ctrl+Shift+O for a new chat, Ctrl+Shift+C to copy the last answer and Esc or the ■ button to stop an answer.
  • Your way: in ⋯ › Settings, choose a dark or light theme (or match your device), the text size, and whether Enter sends. Save any answer to find it later in ⋯ › Saved answers, print or save a whole chat as a PDF from its ⋯ menu, and tap the Next suggestions under an answer to go deeper (an example, a quiz, short notes and more).
  • Editing: attach a file and say what to change. Photos: brighter, black and white, crop square, rotate, blur, sharpen and more happen instantly on your device; Alpaca's image model can remove things, change the background or turn a photo into a cartoon. Videos: trim, speed up or slow down, mute, filters, rotate, add a caption or take a still, all on your device (nothing is uploaded). Word, PowerPoint and Excel files: Alpaca proofreads, rewrites, translates, adds slides or fixes data and gives you a new file (it uses Nova AI's own clean layout, so original pictures and styling aren't kept). Code files: open them in Code Studio, ask Alpaca for changes and see exactly what changed.
  • Talk to Nova: tap the face button next to the mic (or type /talk) to talk with Nova, an animated avatar that listens, answers aloud with a moving mouth and changing expressions, and listens again, hands-free. It can also drive a robot body over Bluetooth, USB or Wi-Fi.
  • Support modes: Wellbeing support (/wellbeing) is a calm listener for low mood, stress and loneliness, and Money help (/money) helps with budgets, debt, loan-app harassment and scams. Anyone in danger gets helplines first (Tele-MANAS 14416, emergency 112). Nova AI is a helper, not a doctor, therapist or financial adviser.
  • Folder workspace: ⋯ › Open a folder (or type /folder) to work on a project folder from your computer, like a coding assistant. Browse and edit its files, ask Alpaca to explain the project or make changes, see exactly what each change does, and approve it before anything is saved. Chrome and Edge on a computer save straight into the folder; other browsers give you the changed files as a .zip. Nothing is ever deleted, and only the files Alpaca needs are sent to it.

How it understands you

Every message goes through four steps, the same building blocks used by large AI systems:

  • Tokenization: your message is split into tokens, small pieces of words, numbers and symbols, in English, Tamil, Hindi and other languages. Tokens measure your usage and decide how much of the conversation Alpaca can read at once.
  • Natural language processing (NLP): Nova AI works out the language you wrote in (including Tanglish and Hinglish), what kind of message it is (a question, maths, code, a picture request…), the key words, and things like numbers, dates and links.
  • Embeddings: a small neural model on your device turns sentences into "meaning vectors", so Nova AI finds answers that mean the same thing even in different words, in its memory, its built-in knowledge and taught documents.
  • Large language model (LLM): Alpaca, built on models such as Google Gemini, writes the answer using everything above: what Nova AI already knows, the language to reply in, and the conversation so far.

How it learns

When Nova AI can't answer something from its own knowledge or open sources, its teacher, Google Gemini, answers, and Nova AI saves that answer to its memory so it knows it next time. If Gemini is busy, the question goes on a learning list. Open Nova AI pages research it when they are idle, using its knowledge base, Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Tamil Wikipedia and Simple English Wikipedia, and every night it studies the whole list and the day's most-read topics. Anyone can also teach it an answer directly. Answers people vote down are dropped.

Nova AI can make mistakes. Check important information, and don't rely on it for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Access

  • Every account starts on the free plan: 10,000 tokens every 5 hours and 100,000 tokens a week (1 token is about 4 letters of your question and the answer). The usage meter at the top right shows what you've used and when it resets.
  • Unlimited members also get Alpaca Vision and the Code School. Tap Ask for unlimited access under your email and the admin reviews it. If you reach the weekly free limit on 5 different weeks, unlimited access turns on by itself.
  • Nova AI is free: there are no payments, ads or subscriptions.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and access level. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us.
  • Your chats: saved to your account so you can open them on any device. Only you can read them.
  • Questions asked: each question is logged (with the account that asked it) so the admin can see what Nova AI should learn. Questions it couldn't answer go on the shared learning list.
  • What you tell Nova AI about yourself (name, class, likes) is kept only in your browser on that device, never in the database. When Alpaca Pro answers, it's included with your question so the answer suits you.
  • What Nova AI learns: answers it researches or people teach it are shared with everyone, together with votes. Please don't teach it private information.

What goes to other services

  • Google Firebase hosts the site and stores the data above.
  • Wikipedia and Wiktionary receive the search words of questions Nova AI looks up.
  • Google Gemini (and, if the admin adds them, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter or GitHub Models) receives Alpaca Pro, Live and Deep questions, attached photos and files, links you paste (YouTube videos and web pages), voiceover scripts for HD voices and picture descriptions, and questions Nova AI couldn't answer by itself. The answer is saved to Nova AI's memory so next time Nova AI answers on its own.
  • Pollinations and AI Horde (free image services) receive the description of a photo you ask Alpaca Vision for, and nothing else.
  • jsDelivr (a public code library) supplies the Python engine the first time you run Python code. Your code runs on your device and isn't sent anywhere.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or show ads.
  • Alpaca Pro, Live and Deep questions and attached files are sent to Google Gemini or the other AI services above (with the last few messages of that chat, so it understands follow-ups). Maths, conversions and its own knowledge base are answered on your device.

Your choices

You can delete any chat from the sidebar. To delete your account and everything linked to it, ask the Nova AI admin.

Terms of use

  • Nova AI is provided free, for learning, as it is, with no guarantee that answers are correct or complete.
  • Use it kindly and lawfully. Don't use it to harass anyone, cheat in exams where it isn't allowed, or make harmful, adult or violent content; Alpaca Vision refuses such requests.
  • Pictures and videos from Alpaca Vision are AI-made. Don't present them as real photos of real events or people.
  • Don't try to break into accounts, the vault or the database. The admin can turn off accounts that misuse Nova AI.
  • These terms may change as Nova AI grows; the date above shows the latest version.
