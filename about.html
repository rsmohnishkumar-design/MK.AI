About MK.ai

MK.ai is a free AI assistant for learning: maths, science, coding, general knowledge and Tamil culture. Its own model answers your questions, and it keeps learning from what people ask.

Models

AnswersAlpacaMK.ai's model, in four modes. Instant: maths, science, conversions and its knowledge base of about 2,650 answers run right in your browser. Pro: writing, explaining, advice, code, apps and office files, using large language models such as Google Gemini. Live: news and anything happening now, with Google Search. Deep: careful step-by-step thinking. Every answer it looks up is saved to its memory, so it keeps getting smarter.
Pictures & videosAlpaca VisionMakes pictures and short videos from a description: HD pictures with Google's image model, realistic photos from a free volunteer-run image service, and logos, cards, charts and patterns drawn by Alpaca itself. For unlimited members; videos are made for the admin only.

Pick a model from the menu under the message box: Alpaca (fast, smart answers), Alpaca Pro (the big model for every answer), Alpaca Pro (Deep thinking) (thinks step by step and checks its work) and Alpaca Vision (pictures; videos are for the admin). The same menu sets the style: Assistant, Tutor, Coder, Writer, Career guide or Friend.

What it can do

  • Maths: arithmetic, fractions, percentages, algebra, equations, derivatives, integrals, limits, matrices, vectors, complex numbers and word problems, with step-by-step working.
  • Science: physics formulas with units, chemistry (molar mass, balancing equations), biology and space.
  • Coding: explanations, examples in many languages, 39 ready-to-run apps (including ATM, traffic light, elevator, vending machine, solar system and projectile simulators), any management system you describe ("make a library management system with book name, author and year" gives a working app with a form, search, sorting, totals and CSV export), and a Code School for beginners.
  • Knowledge: countries, India and Tamil Nadu, history, people, inventions, festivals, careers, full forms, important days, proverbs and the Thirukkural.
  • Photos and documents: attach a photo, a PDF (homework, a bill, a notice) or a text or code file and ask about it, or say "summarise:" and paste a long text.
  • YouTube and web pages: paste a link and ask for a summary, notes or answers from it.
  • Study packs: notes, flashcards, practice questions and an answer key for any topic and class.
  • Run code in the chat: Python and JavaScript run right under the code; HTML apps open in a preview. If something fails, Fix with Alpaca corrects it.
  • Personalities and voice chat: from the ⋯ menu, pick Tutor, Coder, Writer, Career guide or Friend, or talk hands-free.
  • Quizzes: "quiz me on capitals", "quiz me on full forms": multiple-choice questions with instant marking.
  • Feels like a real assistant: answers are typed out, spelling slips are corrected ("Did you mean…?"), and explanations offer more detail or simpler.
  • Office files: "make a ppt on the solar system", "write a word document on pollution", "make an excel sheet for student marks": MK.ai researches the topic, shows a preview, and gives a real .pptx, .docx or .xlsx (Excel sheets come with working formulas).
  • AI voiceovers: "make a voiceover: Welcome to our annual day…" or "make a voiceover about the water cycle" (MK.ai writes the script). Choose a voice, a style (narrator, energetic, calm, deep, cartoon), speed and pitch; on a computer, Record saves the narration as an audio file. The voices come from your device.
  • Talks like a friend: feelings and advice (with helplines if someone is in distress), small talk, stories, riddles, fun facts, quotes, poems, games and everyday choices.
  • Remembers you: tell it your name, class or what you like, and it greets you by name. This stays on your device only.

How it learns

When MK.ai can't answer something from its own knowledge or open sources, its teacher, Google Gemini, answers, and MK.ai saves that answer to its memory so it knows it next time. If Gemini is busy, the question goes on a learning list. Open MK.ai pages research it when they are idle, using its knowledge base, Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Tamil Wikipedia and Simple English Wikipedia, and every night it studies the whole list and the day's most-read topics. Anyone can also teach it an answer directly. Answers people vote down are dropped.

MK.ai can make mistakes. Check important information, and don't rely on it for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Access

  • Every account starts on the free plan: 10,000 tokens every 5 hours and 100,000 tokens a week (1 token is about 4 letters of your question and the answer). The usage meter at the top right shows what you've used and when it resets.
  • Unlimited members also get Alpaca Vision and the Code School. Tap Ask for unlimited access under your email and the admin reviews it. If you reach the weekly free limit on 5 different weeks, unlimited access turns on by itself.
  • MK.ai is free: there are no payments, ads or subscriptions.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and access level. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us.
  • Your chats: saved to your account so you can open them on any device. Only you can read them.
  • Questions asked: each question is logged (with the account that asked it) so the admin can see what MK.ai should learn. Questions it couldn't answer go on the shared learning list.
  • What you tell MK.ai about yourself (name, class, likes) is kept only in your browser on that device, never in the database.
  • What MK.ai learns: answers it researches or people teach it are shared with everyone, together with votes. Please don't teach it private information.

What goes to other services

  • Google Firebase hosts the site and stores the data above.
  • Wikipedia and Wiktionary receive the search words of questions MK.ai looks up.
  • Google Gemini (and, if the admin adds them, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter or GitHub Models) receives Alpaca Pro, Live and Deep questions, attached photos and files, links you paste (YouTube videos and web pages), voiceover scripts for HD voices and picture descriptions, and questions MK.ai couldn't answer by itself. The answer is saved to MK.ai's memory so next time MK.ai answers on its own.
  • AI Horde receives the description of a photo you ask Alpaca Vision for, and nothing else.
  • jsDelivr (a public code library) supplies the Python engine the first time you run Python code. Your code runs on your device and isn't sent anywhere.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or show ads.
  • Alpaca Pro, Live and Deep questions and attached files are sent to Google Gemini or the other AI services above (with the last few messages of that chat, so it understands follow-ups). Maths, conversions and its own knowledge base are answered on your device.

Your choices

You can delete any chat from the sidebar. To delete your account and everything linked to it, ask the MK.ai admin.

Terms of use

  • MK.ai is provided free, for learning, as it is, with no guarantee that answers are correct or complete.
  • Use it kindly and lawfully. Don't use it to harass anyone, cheat in exams where it isn't allowed, or make harmful, adult or violent content; Alpaca Vision refuses such requests.
  • Pictures and videos from Alpaca Vision are AI-made. Don't present them as real photos of real events or people.
  • Don't try to break into accounts, the vault or the database. The admin can turn off accounts that misuse MK.ai.
  • These terms may change as MK.ai grows; the date above shows the latest version.
